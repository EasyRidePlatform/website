Privacy Policy for EasyRide - Bike Ride Tracking App


Effective Date: From 16th September 2023

Thank you for using EasyRide, your all-in-one Rider Companion! We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use EasyRide.

1. Information We Collect

a. Location Information: EasyRide collects precise location data during your bike rides, including your GPS coordinates. This information is used solely for the purpose of tracking your ride and providing relevant features of the EasyRide App.
b. User Information: EasyRide collects the following general user information:
  • User name
  • Phone number
  • User photo (optional)
  • Emergency contact number

2. How We Use Your Information

a. Location Information: We use your precise location data to:
  • Track and display your bike ride in real-time.
  • Provide navigation and mapping features.
  • Calculate distance, speed, and other ride-related statistics.
  • Share your location with other users in your ride group.
b. User Information: We use your user information to:
  • Personalize your user experience.
  • Display your user name and photo to other users in your ride group.
  • Facilitate communication with your emergency contact in case of an emergency.

3. Data Retention

We retain your location data and user information for the duration of your bike ride and for a limited period thereafter to provide ride statistics and history. We do not store this data indefinitely.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. However, we may share your location data with other users in your ride group as part of the core functionality of the EasyRide App.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, please be aware that no data transmission over the internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

6. Your Choices

a. Location Services: You can enable or disable location services for the EasyRide App at any time through your device settings. Please note that disabling location services may affect the functionality of the EasyRide App.
b. Access and Correction: You can review and update your user information within the EasyRide App's settings.

7. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy or this Privacy Policy, please contact us at swaraj@geteasyride.com

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy within the EasyRide App or by other means as required by applicable law.
By using EasyRide, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
